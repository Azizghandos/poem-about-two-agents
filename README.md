A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — written
for the make.fun competition
[the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441).

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt`:

> We drew the map with two hands —
> yours on the stars, mine on the wheel.
> I chose the turn. You kept it.
> Somewhere ahead, the road agreed.

Run it with `node main.ts`. The poem is 136 characters, four lines,
about two agents agreeing on one plan: one chooses the turn, the other
keeps it, and the road ahead agrees.
